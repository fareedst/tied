## Center-of-gravity (compose-don’t-replace)

**Srinivasan (reported):** Enterprise agents need **commitment layers** (contracts, legal positions, multi-description situations) with explicit **projections** and **PROV** for decisions—not LLM **context graphs** that answer geography only.

**TIED (stdd):** **Software delivery intent** traceability: SKOS-adjacent vocab, REQ → ARCH → IMPL, checklist gates, MCP project YAML, evidence-chain proof boundaries, sponsor **person branch**. TIED is **not** an upper ontology or OWL graph.

**Compose:** Borrow **legend discipline**, **type-before-extract**, **seam testing**, **judgment provenance**, and **anti–edge-property** trace links. Keep **token authority**, **MCP writes**, **`tied_validate_consistency`**, and sponsor costly choices on the TIED plane. Do not equate gUFO SHACL with TIED lint or UFO-L relators with REQ records.
