| --- | --- | --- |
| `standard` | `{epoch}-wf-agentstream-wf-NN-{harness}-{modelBucket}` | `1794048393-wf-agentstream-wf-01-cursor-composer25` |
| `legacy-short` | `{epoch}-wf-agentstream-wf-NN` | `1793604353-wf-agentstream-wf-01` |
