# Sponsor lens

**Audience:** Sponsors, product owners, reviewers who approve intent and costly choices—not agents executing the checklist.

---

## What you own vs what the agent instruments

> The sponsor branch holds intent, non-goals, approvals, and **costly choices**. The agent may proceed on **reversible** defaults but must not optimize away sponsor agency.

## Certifiable workflows (without claiming certification)

TIED work is designed so intent, evidence, and gates can support **external appraisal** without the repository claiming third-party certification.

### What “certifiable” means here

[Certification of context-adaptive workflows](../methodologies/certification-of-workflows.md) distinguishes process compliance, capability, and effectiveness. In this repository, **certifiability readiness** means machine-assessable artifacts (spec, mappings, conformance, capability profile, independent assessment, bounded experiments) that support **`ready_for_external_appraisal`** — not **`certified`**, ISO compliance, or CMMI ratings. Stages **1–5** and **7** are in-repo tooling; stages **6** and **8** are human-owned external appraisal.

## Honest limits (read before handoff)

- Valid records ≠ correct product behavior.
- Passing tests ≠ complete edge coverage.
- Readiness **`ready_for_external_appraisal`** ≠ certification or standard compliance.
- Reference mappings do not establish ISO/CMMI conformance claims.

---

## EDSE in one paragraph

**Evidence-Driven Situational Engineering (EDSE)** composes with TIED: characterize context → select practices (CASE) → execute the checklist → collect verification evidence. See [context-adaptive-engineering.md](../../tied-project/vocab/context-adaptive-engineering.md).

Engineers and agents carry the daily loop; sponsors adjudicate **approved** intent on brownfield engagements and sign off on evidence envelopes when the Tracker requires them.
